Protect your received files

A passkey is like a key that only you have, kept on your phone or computer. It keeps the files people send you private.

You'll make two. The second one is a spare key, in case you lose the first.

  1. Your first passkey
  2. Your spare passkey

For your safety, first confirm with the passkey you already made. Then you'll create the spare one. You'll be asked twice. That's normal.

Where do you want to keep this passkey?

You'll unlock it with your fingerprint, your face, or the PIN of this computer.

You'll scan a QR code with your phone's camera.

Before you start:

  • Turn on Bluetooth on your phone and on this computer.
  • Both need to be connected to the internet.
  • On Android, save the passkey in Google Password Manager. Microsoft Authenticator can't be used for Dropr.
  • On iPhone, save it in Passwords (iCloud Keychain).

A small key that you plug in or tap. Only if you have one.

Your browser shows all its options, such as a password manager.

When you click the button, your browser will show you its own options. Choose where to keep your passkey there.

On this browser, a security key (a small key that you plug in) works best. You can also open this page in Chrome, Edge or Safari.

Using your phone? What you need

Without a passkey, files sent to you can't be opened. That's why the spare one matters.
